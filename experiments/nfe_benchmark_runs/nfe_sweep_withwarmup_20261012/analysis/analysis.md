# Fixed-NFE dots.tts benchmark analysis

Source: [`results.csv`](../results.csv)
Prompt ID: `short`
Target text: The small red bird landed on the garden fence.
Measured generations: 45 across 9 NFE values, with 5 measured repeats per NFE.

## Benchmark configuration

The saved result rows report `dots-studio/dots.tts-soar`, CUDA, bfloat16, Euler, seed 42, guidance scale 1.2, speaker scale 1.5, max generation length 80, max sequence length 2048, and optimization disabled. The associated run summary reports one unmeasured warmup per NFE. Warmups are not present in the measured results CSV.

This is one target prompt repeated across NFE settings. It does not estimate performance across the 54-prompt manifest. The earlier sweep was reported to repeatedly speak the shared reference phrase. These timing results are a single-prompt performance trace, not evidence of correct prompt adherence or speech quality.

## Behavior classification

- **Latency rises strongly with NFE:** median latency grows from 6.96s at NFE 4 to 36.09s at NFE 32, a 5.19x increase. The least-squares trend across the per-NFE medians is 1.033 seconds per additional NFE with R² 0.996, close to linear for this prompt and configuration.
- **Real-time boundary:** median RTF is below 1 only at 4 NFE. NFE 6 is close to parity at 1.01; NFE 32 is 3.96x real time.
- **Local plateau:** median latency is effectively unchanged between NFE 10 and 12 (13.776s vs 13.773s), while median audio duration falls from 9.12s to 8.16s.
- **Output duration is tightly clustered:** observed duration range is 8.16s to 9.12s for target text 'The small red bird landed on the garden fence.'. The maximum duration occurs in 25/45 measured rows. This concentration warrants checking generation termination and listening to the WAVs, especially given the reported reference-phrase repetition.
- **Repeat variability:** NFE 8 and 10 have the widest latency spread; other settings are comparatively stable across the five recorded runs. With five repeats and one prompt, these spreads describe this run only.

## Limits

No new GPU generations were started while preparing this report. These plots use the existing five-repeat single-prompt run. The previous full 54-prompt sweep is not pooled into this analysis, and no claim is made that the audio content follows the target text. The current local environment has no `nvidia-smi`, and Matplotlib/NumPy are not installed. The SVGs and statistics are generated by the included Python standard-library script.
